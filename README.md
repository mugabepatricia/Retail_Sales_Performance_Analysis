# Retail Sales Performance Analysis 2024

## Overview
A comprehensive sales performance analysis conducted for a retail organisation operating across four regions in 2024. This project examines revenue distribution, product category performance, and sales representative target achievement.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which regions are driving the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best overall and by region?
- Are sales representatives meeting their annual targets?

## Tools Used
- **R** — data manipulation and analysis
- **dplyr** — data wrangling
- **ggplot2** — data visualisation
- **R Markdown** — report generation
- **RPubs** — report publishing

## Key Findings
- The Southern region recorded the highest total sales in 2024
- Electronics is the dominant category across all regions except the East
- The East region showed a unique preference for Furniture — a notable consumer behaviour anomaly
- All 10 sales representatives met their annual targets
